Delete your AI FitSense account

You can delete your AI FitSense account and all of your data, inside the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open AI FitSense and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Tap Permanently delete my account.

Your account and data are deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: By email (no app access needed)

If you uninstalled the app or can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and data.

Email a deletion request

  • Send it from the email address you signed up with, so we can match it to your account.
  • Never include your password.
  • We will delete your account and data within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password credential)
  • Your profile (name, age, gender, height, weight, activity level, fitness goal, and fitness-readiness answers)
  • Your workout plans and workout history
  • Your diet plans
  • Your weight and progress logs
  • Your water intake logs

What may remain

  • Routine server logs kept for a limited time by our hosting provider, which can include your account ID but not your profile or activity data.
  • Crash reports, which are not linked to your account.
  • Anything we are required to keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

AI FitSense does not store camera images or video; pose detection runs only on your device. See the Privacy Policy in the app (Profile tab) for full details.
